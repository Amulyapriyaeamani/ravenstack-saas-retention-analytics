## Role & Context

I worked as a Product Analyst at RavenStack, a SaaS platform preparing for its public launch.

My responsibility was to analyze user behavior, subscription patterns, feature usage, and support interactions to uncover key drivers of churn, retention, and revenue performance.

The objective was to provide actionable insights to improve product adoption, reduce churn, and optimize revenue before launch.

## Business Objective

The primary objective of this analysis is to evaluate RavenStack’s pre-launch performance by identifying the key factors influencing customer churn, retention, and revenue growth.

By analyzing user behavior, feature usage patterns, support interactions, and subscription dynamics, the goal is to uncover actionable insights that can improve product adoption, reduce churn risk, and drive sustainable revenue growth prior to launch.
